# Capability-realisation experience

Lily begins with intent: **What do you want your company to be able to do?** OmniSeed OS then projects runtime-calculated coverage, the selected realisation, missing requirements, governed plans, and attention. It does not ask a founder to assemble agents, connectors, workflows, schedules, or providers manually.

The Capability view progressively discloses those implementation resources. A missing human authority is shown as a precise requirement, never as a vague request to “hire someone”. Deep resource and infrastructure inspection remain available to operators.

Text and browser voice input enter the same Lily intent resolver and Omniform-derived operation registry. Lily may explain or request a plan; policy and authorization govern mutation. Demo mode remains read-only and does not claim that its example resources were provisioned.
